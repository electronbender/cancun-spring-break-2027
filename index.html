Questions

FAQ

Do I need a passport?

Yes — a valid passport book is required to fly to Mexico. Check that it doesn't expire during your trip, and apply early: processing can take several weeks.

How old do I need to be?

Travelers must be 18+ to book. The legal drinking age in Mexico is 18; some events may have their own age rules.

Are flights included?

Packages are priced land-only so you can use any airline or points. We can add flights from most major U.S. airports — use the “Flights from my city” prompt.

Can my group pay separately?

Yes. Each traveler gets their own payment link, and the room is held as long as each person's $99 deposit is in.

What's the cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable for 14 days after booking. After that, you can switch to a travel credit up to 60 days before departure. Trip protection is available at checkout.

Is Cancún safe for spring break?

The Hotel Zone is the most visited area of Cancún and has a strong tourist-police presence. Stick with your group, use official taxis or our transport, watch your drinks, and check the latest U.S. State Department advisory before you go.
